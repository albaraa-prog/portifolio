Final‑year project · AI research

Geolab

Point your camera at a rock. Know what it is.

Three models work in stages. The first asks whether it's a rock at all, the next finds the family, and the last names the exact type. Together they reach 95.56% accuracy. I don't trust black boxes, so every result comes with a Grad‑CAM heatmap showing which part of the stone the model looked at. The research behind it was published at ICADAAI 2026.

  • EfficientNet‑B7
  • ResNet‑50
  • Grad‑CAM
  • Flask
  • Supabase
  • Flutter
AI · Health

Dia ديا

A diabetes nurse you can talk to, in Omani Arabic or English.

Say "my sugar is 145 before lunch" and Dia logs it. Snap a plate of majboos and she estimates the carbs. Photograph a lab sheet and she explains your HbA1c. Or just call her and talk hands‑free. The safety rules for lows, highs and insulin doses are written in code, so the AI can never override them.

Realtime · Privacy

Void

Talk, chat and share your screen, and keep it between you and the people you're talking to.

Servers, roles, text and voice channels, friends and DMs, on web, Windows, macOS, Android and iOS. There's no app server at all. Voice runs peer‑to‑peer over WebRTC, and messages are sealed on your device with X25519 and AES‑256‑GCM, so the database only ever holds ciphertext.

Game · Oman

العريف Al‑Areef

Bring the falaj water home before the clock runs out.

In Omani villages, the areef is the person who shares out the falaj water. In my game, you take his job. Rotate the channel tiles to carry water from the well to the palms in as few moves as you can. It's the first piece of the Oman game I've wanted to make since I started.
